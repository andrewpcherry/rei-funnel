The four ways

Four structures, and the situation each one is actually for.

These are not four flavours of the same deal. They pay you differently, they close differently, and they suit opposite situations. Anybody who only offers you one of them is telling you about their business, not about your house.

Option one

We buy it for cash

We purchase the property outright. You get the whole amount at closing, you pick the date, and there is no lender who can change their mind in week five.

Best when
The house needs work, the date matters, or you need the money in one piece.
The trade
The price sits below what a perfect retail sale would fetch. We show you how we got to it.
You need
Enough equity for the discount to still leave you whole.
Option two

You carry the financing

You sell us the house and we pay you monthly instead of all at once. Because we are not paying cash, the total price is usually well above a cash offer, sometimes close to full market value.

Best when
You own it outright or nearly so, and a monthly cheque suits you better than a lump sum.
The trade
You get paid over time rather than at closing, and you are relying on us to keep paying.
Worth asking
Your accountant. Spreading payments across years can change what you owe in tax.
Option three

We take over the payments

Your existing loan stays in place and we take over making the payments on it. This is the route for people with little equity, or who are behind and want out from under the payment rather than out of a house they have money in.

Best when
You owe close to what it is worth, the rate is low, or you are behind and the clock is running.
Be clear on this
The loan stays in your name until it is paid off or refinanced. That is the whole risk, and we will put the protections in writing before you sign anything.
You should
Have your own attorney read it. We will wait.
Option four

You list it on the open market

Sometimes the honest answer is that you will do better with an agent, and we would rather say so than talk you into the wrong sale.

Best when
The house shows well, you have real equity, and you can wait sixty to ninety days.
The trade
Commission, repairs the buyer's lender demands, showings, and a sale that can still collapse at the appraisal.
We will
Tell you when this is your better move, and point you at an agent if you want one.

Straight answers

The questions everybody asks first.

Does answering these questions commit me to anything?+

No. It is not a listing agreement, not a contract, and not a commitment to sell. You can go through the whole thing, read what we send you, and never speak to us again.

Why would I take payments instead of cash?+

Because the total is usually a lot higher. When we do not have to raise the money up front, we can pay far closer to what the house is really worth. If you own the house outright and you do not need a lump sum on a particular date, this is often the route that pays you the most. It is also the route where you should have your own attorney involved, because you are relying on payments continuing.

What does taking over my payments actually mean?+

Your mortgage stays where it is and we take over paying it. You stop being responsible for finding the money each month. The part you must understand: the loan remains in your name until it is paid off or refinanced, so the protections written into the agreement are the whole ball game. We put them in writing and we expect you to have your own attorney read them.

Are you going to lowball me?+

Our cash number sits below what a perfect retail sale would fetch and we will say so plainly. That is also exactly why we show you the other three routes, because for a lot of people the cash offer is not the one that pays the most. What we will not do is quote a high number to get a contract signed and then chip away at it before closing.

What if I owe more than the house is worth?+

That does not end the conversation. It usually rules out a cash purchase, because there is no room in it, and it is often exactly the situation where taking over the payments works. There are also cases where the honest answer is that selling is not your best move, and we will tell you that too.

Do I have to clean it out or fix anything?+

No. Take what you want and leave the rest, including furniture, cars, and whatever is in the garage. We deal with it after closing. This comes up most with inherited properties and it is never a problem.

I already have an agent. Should I still do this?+

Not yet. While you are under a listing agreement we stay out of it, both out of courtesy and because depending on your contract it can create a problem for you. Come back if the listing expires.

Who actually sees my information?+

We do. We are the buyer, not a lead site. Your details are not sold on to a list of investors, which is what happens on most forms that look like this one.
